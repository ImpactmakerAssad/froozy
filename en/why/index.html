The cold trick

How to make fruit crack

No oven, no additives, no tricks. Just cold, vacuum and patience. An apple becomes a crisp, a strawberry becomes a crackle, and nothing gets lost along the way.

  1. 01

    Fresh fruit

    Two kinds of fruit per bag. Nothing else, because nothing else is needed.

  2. 02

    Frozen to −80 degrees

    Colder than a polar night. Every drop of flavour freezes right where it lives.

  3. 03

    Vacuum pulls the water out

    The ice lifts straight out of the fruit. No heat, nothing lost.

  4. 04

    Flavour, crunch and power stay

    What matters is left behind, only lighter and more intense. We call it crrrunch.
